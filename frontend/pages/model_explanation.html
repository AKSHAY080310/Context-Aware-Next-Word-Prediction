UNDERSTANDING THE MODEL

How does prediction work?

A look at how written text is transformed into numerical representations and processed by the LSTM model.

01

Input text

The user enters a sentence or phrase.

The future of artificial intelligence
02

Tokenization

NLTK word_tokenize divides the text into tokens.

["The", "future", "of", "artificial", "intelligence"]
03

Numerical encoding

Every known token is converted into its vocabulary ID. Unknown words are assigned the UNK ID.

[ID, ID, ID, ID, ID]
04

Context preparation

The model accepts the most recent 10 token IDs. Shorter inputs are padded on the left.

Fixed input length: 10
05

LSTM processing

The embedding layer converts token IDs into vectors. The LSTM processes the sequence and learns relationships across the context.

Embedding → LSTM → Dropout → Dense
06

Next-word prediction

The final softmax layer produces probabilities over the vocabulary. We rank these probabilities to show the most likely next words.

Softmax → Top-K predictions
