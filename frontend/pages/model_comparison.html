What these metrics mean

Top-1 Accuracy

Correct next word is the highest-ranked prediction.

Top-5 Accuracy

Correct next word appears among the five highest-ranked predictions.

Top-10 Accuracy

Correct next word appears among the ten highest-ranked predictions.

Test Loss

Average prediction loss; lower is generally better. Compare values only when evaluation methods are aligned.
