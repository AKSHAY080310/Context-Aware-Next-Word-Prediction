What these metrics mean

Top-1 Accuracy

Percentage of test examples where the highest-probability predicted word exactly matches the actual next word.

Top-5 Accuracy

Percentage of test examples where the actual next word appears among the five highest-ranked predictions.

Top-10 Accuracy

Percentage of test examples where the actual next word appears among the ten highest-ranked predictions.

Test Loss

Measures how well the model assigns probability to the actual next word. Lower is generally better.
